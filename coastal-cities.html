Research question and findings

Question: How do published historic relative sea level trends differ across selected U.S. coastal tide gauge stations?

Method: Extract published NOAA historic trend estimates, record observation periods and confidence intervals, then rank and compare them using a CSV, SQL queries and a Python script.

Finding: Norfolk's Sewells Point has the highest historic trend in this five station sample at 4.84 mm per year. Honolulu has the lowest at 1.57 mm per year. These results do not establish the timing or footprint of future flooding.

Limitation: Observation periods differ. NOAA warns against extrapolating historic linear trends as future projections. Detailed flood risk requires scenario projections and local elevation mapping.
