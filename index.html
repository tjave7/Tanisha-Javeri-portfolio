Certificate of completion

NASA ARSET · Fundamentals of Remote Sensing

Training covered how satellites collect information about Earth, including sensor types, satellite orbits, spatial and temporal resolution, and ways remote sensing supports environmental monitoring.

What I learned and can explain from the training:

  • How passive sensors detect naturally occurring energy and active sensors send signals to measure Earth features.
  • Why spatial, spectral, temporal and radiometric resolution affect which environmental questions a dataset can answer.
  • How satellite orbit and revisit time influence the availability of observations.
  • How remote sensing can help investigate vegetation, land cover and environmental change, while field validation and data quality checks remain important.
